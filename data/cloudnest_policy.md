# CloudNest Subscription and Refund Policy

*Last updated: 1 September 2026. CloudNest is a fictional company created for this assessment.*

## 1. Plans and Pricing

| Plan | Price | Billing |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly | $20 per month | Charged every month |
| Annual | $200 per year | Charged once per year |

Both plans include 100 GB of cloud storage and email support.

## 2. Free Trial

New customers get a 7-day free trial. No charge is made during the trial. If the customer does not cancel before the trial ends, the selected plan is charged automatically.

## 3. Refund Rules

1. **Within 14 days of payment:** the customer receives a full refund on either plan.
2. **Monthly plan, after 14 days:** no refund is given. The customer can cancel and keep access until the end of the paid month.
3. **Annual plan, after 14 days:** the customer receives a prorated refund for each full unused month. A 10% processing fee is deducted from the refund amount.
4. Partial months are not refunded.
5. Refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days.

**Prorated refund formula (Annual plan):**
Refund = (Annual price / 12) x (number of full unused months) x 0.90

## 4. How to Cancel

Go to **Settings > Billing > Cancel Subscription**. Cancellation takes effect immediately, and the customer keeps access until the end of the paid period.

## 5. Contact

Refund requests must be sent by email to **support@cloudnest.example**. Requests are answered within 2 business days.
